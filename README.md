# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Sales Analysis Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard analyzes sales performance, customers, products, order quantity, shipping performance, ratings, and business segments to provide clear and actionable insights.

The project focuses on transforming raw sales data into an interactive business intelligence solution that helps users understand sales trends and customer behavior.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and order performance.
- Track sales and payment trends over time.
- Analyze customer segments and customer levels.
- Understand product and order performance.
- Evaluate shipping performance.
- Analyze customer ratings.
- Identify important business patterns and trends.
- Build an interactive and user-friendly dashboard.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI**
- **Power Query**

- **Data Modeling**

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Start Page
A navigation page designed to provide easy access to the different sections of the dashboard.

### 2. Overview
Provides a high-level view of:

- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Customers by Province
- Quantity by Market and Segment
- Customer Levels
- Quantity by Segment
- Quantity trends over time
- Geographic distribution of customers

### 3. Orders & Products
Focuses on order and product-related analysis and helps explore sales performance across different dimensions.

### 4. Shipping
Analyzes shipping performance including:

- Average Shipping Days
- Shipping Modes
- Order Ratings
- Cost and Payment trends
- Running sales/cost trends

### 5. Customer Info
Provides customer-level analysis and geographic/customer segmentation.

### 6. Sales Details
Provides detailed order-level information including:

- Order ID
- Product Name
- Category
- Customer ID
- Customer Name
- Order Date
- Quantity
- Cost

# 🔍 Key Insights

## 💰 Sales & Payment

- Total **Cost:** approximately **13.91M**
- Total **Payment:** approximately **31.37M**
- The highest recorded yearly payment was in **2024**, at approximately **9.56M**.
- Payment increased substantially from 2021 to 2024.
- The dashboard shows a clear upward trend in cumulative cost/payment across the analyzed years.

---

## 📦 Orders & Quantity

- Total Orders: approximately **50.86K**
- Total Quantity: approximately **177K**

### Quantity by Segment

| Segment | Quantity | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Consumer | ~92K | 52% |
| Corporate | ~53K | 30% |
| Home Office | ~32K | 18% |

The **Consumer segment accounts for the largest share of total quantity**.

---

## 👥 Customer Levels

The customer distribution is divided into four levels:

| Customer Level | Share |
|---|---:|
| V1 | 58% |
| V2 | 21% |
| V3 | 13% |
| VIP | 8% |

V1 customers represent the largest customer group in the analyzed data.

---

## 🚚 Shipping Performance

Average shipping time varies significantly by shipping mode:

| Shipping Mode | Average Days |
|---|---:|
| Standard Class | 6.3 |
| Second Class | 4.3 |
| First Class | 3.4 |
| Same Day | 0.3 |

Same Day shipping has the shortest average delivery time, while Standard Class has the longest average delivery time among the displayed shipping modes.

---

## ⭐ Customer Ratings

The dashboard classifies orders into **Good** and **Bad** ratings.

- Good Rating: **87.48%**
- Bad Rating: **12.52%**

This indicates that the majority of recorded orders fall within the Good rating category.

---

## 📅 Yearly Performance

The dashboard compares yearly Cost and Payment:

| Year | Cost | Payment |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2021 | ~2.49M | ~3.97M |
| 2022 | ~2.93M | ~6.88M |
| 2023 | ~3.75M | ~7.61M |
| 2024 | ~4.73M | ~9.56M |

The data shows an increase in both cost and payment across the displayed years.

---

## 📈 Business Insights

1. **Consumer customers generate the largest share of quantity**, contributing around 52% of total quantity.
2. **V1 customers represent the largest customer-level group**, accounting for around 58%.
3. **2024 records the highest yearly cost and payment** among the displayed years.
4. **Payment increased considerably between 2021 and 2024**, indicating strong growth in the analyzed sales activity.
5. **Standard Class shipping has the highest average delivery time**, while Same Day is significantly faster.
6. The majority of orders have a **Good rating (87.48%)**.
7. The geographic analysis shows **Riyadh** as the province with the highest displayed customer count.
8. The dashboard combines sales, customers, products, shipping, and geographic information to provide a broader view of business performance.
